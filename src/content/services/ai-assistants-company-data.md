ZanamTech builds AI assistants that put company knowledge to work. Staff or customers ask questions in plain
language and receive answers drawn from your approved documents and systems, with citations back to the source.

Assistants run on managed AI services inside your own cloud tenant, respect existing access rights and are measured
against an evaluation set, so quality is tracked rather than assumed.

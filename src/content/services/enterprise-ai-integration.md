ZanamTech embeds practical, secure artificial intelligence into core enterprise operations through private Large
Language Models and vector-based Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

Designed for data-sensitive environments, these integrations automate document queries, internal workflows and
customer support while keeping proprietary information inside the organization's own infrastructure rather than
sending it to public third-party APIs.

# 4. Engagement & Custom Proposal Process

ZanamTech adheres to a consultative B2B engagement model designed to deliver custom enterprise solutions matched to client technical requirements. To maintain engineering standards and enterprise value, we do not publish fixed price lists or hourly commodity rates. Every engagement begins with a direct, architectural assessment to establish clear scope boundaries, SLA commitments, and measurable outcomes.

### Phase 1: Technical Audit & Infrastructure Discovery
Every potential partnership initiates with a technical audit of the client’s existing environment. Our senior architects analyze cloud architecture configurations, database replication setups, security perimeters, CI/CD pipelines, and monthly resource expenditure to identify bottlenecks, vulnerabilities, and cost-reduction opportunities.

### Phase 2: Custom Proposal & Statement of Work (SOW)
Following the audit, ZanamTech authors a detailed technical Statement of Work (SOW) and custom engagement proposal. This document outlines:
* Architectural specifications and deliverables.
* Deployment milestones and delivery schedules.
* Service Level Agreements (SLAs) including uptime guarantees and security benchmarks.
* Transparent project pricing structured under a **Fixed-Scope Setup** for one-time engineering deployments or a **Monthly Retainer** for ongoing managed services.

### Phase 3: Onboarding & Zero-Downtime Implementation
Upon proposal execution, senior cloud and DevSecOps engineers take direct responsibility for implementation. Deployments follow strict IaC protocols, staging validation, and zero-downtime cutover procedures to ensure operational continuity.

### Phase 4: Continuous 24/7 Support & Managed Retainer
For clients transitioning to ongoing managed operations, ZanamTech assumes 24/7 accountability for hosting stability, proactive monitoring, security updates, and incident resolution (Service 9). Regular monthly performance reviews ensure infrastructure evolves alongside client growth.

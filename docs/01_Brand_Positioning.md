# ZanamTech Brand Positioning

> **Source of truth:** `docs/03_Master_Plan_v3.md` (approved 2026-10-11). This document is derived
> from it and replaces the earlier positioning brief. Constitution v2.0.0 (Principle III) governs
> every claim on the website.

## 1. Positioning statement

"ZanamTech is a Cloud, DevOps & AI engineering firm. We run, secure and modernise the
cloud platforms growing businesses depend on, and automate the work that slows them down, with
documented runbooks, transparent reporting and no lock-in."

ZanamTech sells accountability for a client's whole cloud and delivery platform, not a menu of small
tasks.

## 2. Category

A Managed Cloud & DevOps partner plus an engineering studio for SaaS and practical AI. It is not a
dev shop, a staff-augmentation marketplace or a design agency.

**Flagship:** the Managed Cloud & DevOps retainer. It brings recurring revenue and is the work
ZanamTech already delivers every day: cloud operations, Dev/QA/UAT/Prod environments, CI/CD
pipelines, backups, and repository governance and SOPs.

**Platforms:** AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud are treated equally. Wherever a cloud is named in general copy, all three are named, in that order (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud). Real past scenarios keep the cloud they actually ran on.

## 6. How engagements are described

The website describes the process a client gets, never who sits behind it.

- **Cadence:** kickoff and RACI · weekly status · monthly service report (retainers) · quarterly
  architecture review · runbooks and full handover.
- **Access model:** least privilege, read-only first, MFA, client-owned accounts and repositories,
  NDA available before discovery, DPA on request.
- **AI-tool policy:**
  - AI tools are used only under business terms that exclude training on customer data.
  - No client secrets, credentials or personal data are pasted into AI tools.
  - Every AI-assisted change is reviewed before it reaches a client environment.
  - A client can opt out of AI-assisted work in its SOW.

### Approved wording

- "Every engagement runs on a written SOW, a kickoff, weekly status reports and a monthly service review."
- "You get documented runbooks, architecture diagrams and a full handover. No lock-in."
- "We work in your accounts and repositories with least-privilege access and MFA. NDA available
  before discovery."
- "As your scope grows, delivery scales with vetted engineers working to the same standards and
  access controls."
- **Time commitments (the only ones allowed):** "Written proposal within 2 business days" and "We
  reply within 1 business day".

"We" is fine for the company. A team photo or an "our team" page is not, until there is a team.

### Banned wording (enforced by the content lint)

- **Team structure:** "direct access to senior engineers", "our senior engineering team", "PM-led",
  "project-manager-led", "one accountable lead", any headcount figure, first-person "I".
- **Low-ticket framing:** "gig", "hourly" (as a rate), "packages from". Technical frequencies such as "hourly encrypted backups" are allowed.
- **Over-promising:** "guarantee", "guaranteed uptime", "zero downtime", "zero data loss", "100%",
  unqualified "24/7", "on-call engineers 24/7".
- **Unearned status:** "certified" (until held), Ltd/Inc/LLC (until registered), "our offices".
- **AI hosting:** "local LLM", "on-premise LLM", "fine-tuned model".
- **Time figures:** clock times (e.g. "10:00"), time-zone labels (PKT, GMT, BST, EST, PST, GST,
  UTC±n, etc.), and minute or hour figures (e.g. "30-minute", "2 hours"). Allowed: the phrase
  "time-zone overlap", durations in weeks, and the two business-day commitments.
- **Also prohibited:** any years-of-experience claim, public prices, and competitor names.

## 7. Proof and trust

- **Results:** past-project % results stay, worded as achieved results (e.g. "cut deployment time by
  50% for a B2B SaaS platform"), under the heading "Selected results from past engagements", with
  no per-card credit line and this footnote: "Results from past engagements; outcomes vary by
  environment. Commitments are defined per Statement of Work."
- **Scenarios:** every scenario is recorded as real or illustrative. Real, anonymised projects keep
  their numbers; illustrative examples ("Example engagement") carry none.
- **Testimonials, client logos, named case studies, project counts and badges:** real and permissioned
  (verified, with a permission date and an evidence note), or absent. Never written by ZanamTech.
- **Launches with:**
  - results from delivered projects
  - proof-by-artefact (reference architecture, SOP template, sample monthly report, all marked
    "Sample")
  - the technology marquee ("Technologies we work with")
  - ZanamTech-controlled commitments
  - a real founder profile
  - a Trust page and security.txt
  - three Insights articles
  - cited industry data

## 8. Location and company presentation

- The website never says "remote-first" (banned by the content lint) and shows no address or city. Delivery location is stated
  plainly on calls and in proposals.
- **Never:**
  - a virtual-office address presented as a headquarters
  - "offices in London/Dubai"
  - stock office photos
  - a foreign phone number presented as an office
- The registered legal name and city are added once a company is registered.

## 9. Never sell

- Logo or branding work.
- WordPress or Shopify sites.
- Low-ticket gigs or hourly staff augmentation.
- 24/7 SOC/MDR.
- ERP implementations.
- Mobile games, AR/VR or blockchain.
- SOC 2/ISO certification projects (readiness support only).
- Custom ML model training.
- Self-hosted LLM hosting.
